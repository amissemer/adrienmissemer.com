### A private tool, not a service

This is a command-line tool that I run on my own Mac to track my family's net worth and cash flow. It is not published, not sold, and not offered as a service to anyone.

There is no account to create, nothing to subscribe to, and no product being marketed. The only people who use it are me and consenting members of my family. If you are reading this page, it is almost certainly because you are reviewing the application rather than using it.

### Read-only account information

The application reads account information only: balances and transaction history.

It never initiates payments, never moves money, and never modifies anything at the bank. There is no payment initiation functionality and none is planned.

### Who does what

I am not a bank, a financial institution, a payment service provider or a licensed intermediary of any kind. I am an individual running software on a personal computer.

The regulated part — connecting to the bank and retrieving account data under PSD2 — is performed by [Enable Banking Oy](https://enablebanking.com/), a Finnish licensed account information service provider (AISP). Their own terms and their supervision by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority govern that part of the flow. The bank that holds the account is BoursoBank in France, and the account holder's agreement with the bank continues to apply.

Access is authorised by the account holder through the bank's own strong customer authentication, and it can be revoked at any time, either in the bank's interface or by deleting the session through Enable Banking.

### The figures are informational only

Anything the application displays — balances, totals, net worth, categorised spending — is informational.

It is not financial, tax, investment or legal advice. It is not an authoritative account statement. Figures may be stale, incomplete, mis-categorised, or simply wrong because of a bug, a failed refresh, or a change in what the bank returns.

**The bank's own records govern.** If what the application shows and what the bank shows disagree, the bank is right and the application is wrong. Do not rely on these figures for anything that matters — a tax filing, a transaction, a decision about money — without checking them against the bank directly.
